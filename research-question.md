# Research question

**Primary question.** On a prospectively declared, licensed, rectangular adjusted-price
panel, does long-only shrinkage minimum-variance allocation reduce final-holdout
annualized volatility relative to equal weight without producing a worse maximum
drawdown after a declared flat transaction cost?

**Null hypothesis.** Shrinkage minimum variance does not improve the joint
(volatility, max-drawdown) risk profile versus equal weight on the untouched final
holdout after costs.

**Non-questions (explicitly out of scope).** Absolute profitability, trading alpha,
learned predictors, shorting, leverage, and live execution quality.
